Guidelines

Tournament Rules

Sabhi teams in rules ko follow karna zaroori hai

🏏 Match Rules

  • Har match 8-8 overs ka hoga (T8 Format)
  • 8 players har side se khelenge
  • Toss se decide hoga ki pehle batting ya bowling
  • Powerplay: First overs mein sirf 2 fielders allowed boundary ke bahar
  • Ek bowler maximum 3 overs bowl kar sakta hai
  • No-ball pe free hit milegi
  • Tie hone par Super Over hoga

👥 Team Rules

  • Har team mein exactly 8 players + 3 substitutes allowed
  • Playing XI match se 30 min pehle submit karna hoga
  • Koi bhi player sirf ek team ke liye khelega
  • Age limit: 16+ years
  • Proper cricket kit (white dress) zaroori nhi hai
  • Playing kit mein team ka naam hona chahiye koi zaroorinhi hai

📋 Registration Rules

  • Entry fee ₹1100 per team (non-refundable)
  • Registration deadline: June 8, 2026
  • Name mandatory hai sabhi players ka
  • Team captain ka phone number verified hoga
  • Maximum 32 teams participate kar sakti hain
  • Pehle aao pehle khelo basis nhi hoga ye decision hamri managment team kregi

⚖️ Code of Conduct

  • Umpire ka decision final hoga
  • Kisi bhi player ke saath gali-galoch allowed nahi
  • Arguing with umpire = immediate dismissal
  • Match samay par shuru karna zaroori hai
  • 10 min delay = batting pe penalty runs
  • Organizers ka decision har dispute mein final hoga
